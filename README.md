## About

I design and ship end-to-end systems — from LLM-backed backends to the interfaces on top of them. My work centers on making AI genuinely useful in production: agents that complete real tasks, retrieval pipelines that stay accurate at scale, and full-stack platforms built to hold up under real users, not just demos.

I work across the entire stack by choice, not necessity — architecture, backend, frontend, infrastructure, and security are one continuous problem to me, not separate disciplines handed off between people.

## Focus Areas

- **AI Agents & Autonomous Systems** — task-completing agents, tool orchestration, multi-step reasoning pipelines
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — knowledge systems built for accuracy and scale, not just semantic search demos
- **LLM Integration** — OpenAI and local/open-weight models, evaluated and deployed on their actual tradeoffs
- **Full-Stack SaaS** — React on the frontend, Django or FastAPI on the backend, built to ship
- **DevSecOps & Cloud Infrastructure** — security and reliability treated as first-class requirements, not afterthoughts

## Currently Deepening

Data engineering pipelines · advanced agent orchestration (LangGraph, MCP) · distributed systems at scale
